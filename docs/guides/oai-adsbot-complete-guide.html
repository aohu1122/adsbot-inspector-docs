What is OAI-AdsBot?

OAI-AdsBot is the web crawler OpenAI sends to every landing page submitted through ChatGPT Ads. When you create a campaign in the Ads Manager, OpenAI visits your page to verify three things before your ad can serve:

  1. Safety & policy compliance — does the page violate OpenAI's advertising policies?
  2. Content relevance — does the page match your ad copy and context hints?
  3. Quality signals — is the page real, reachable content (not an error page or an empty shell)?

It announces itself with this user-agent string:

Mozilla/5.0 AppleWebKit/537.36 (KHTML, like Gecko); compatible; OAI-AdsBot/1.0; +https://openai.com/adsbot

OpenAI formally added OAI-AdsBot to its public crawler documentation in April 2026, as ChatGPT Ads rolled out from its February 2026 US launch to 40+ countries. Since May 2026 the platform is fully self-serve with no minimum spend — which means thousands of first-time advertisers are now meeting this crawler, often without knowing it exists.

How OAI-AdsBot differs from GPTBot and OAI-SearchBot

OpenAI operates several crawlers, and conflating them causes real mistakes:

Key takeaway: OAI-AdsBot only visits pages you explicitly submit. If your ad is stuck in review or was silently rejected, this crawler's experience of your page is the first thing to investigate.

Why your page may be invisible to OAI-AdsBot

The most common failure mode isn't a policy violation — it's that the crawler never saw your real page. Three culprits account for most cases:

1. Your WAF or CDN blocks it

Cloudflare, Akamai, DataDome, and Imperva all classify unknown crawlers as bot traffic. Because OpenAI publishes no IP range list for OAI-AdsBot (unlike its other crawlers), bot-protection defaults can challenge or block it. OpenAI's Help Center explicitly requires that landing pages not block OAI-AdsBot — a blocked crawl generally means a failed review.

2. Your page is a JavaScript-only shell

If your site is a client-side rendered SPA, a crawler that doesn't execute JavaScript sees an empty skeleton — no headline, no copy, nothing to score for relevance. Treat OAI-AdsBot as if it does not run JS: your key content (title, meta description, H1, body copy) must be present in the server-delivered HTML.

3. robots.txt disallows it

OpenAI's guidance for advertisers is to allow both OAI-AdsBot and OAI-SearchBot in robots.txt. A blanket Disallow: / or an overly broad bot rule can take your own landing page out of the running.

Pre-submission checklist

  • ✓ Landing page returns HTTP 200 to OAI-AdsBot's user-agent (no challenge page, no 403)
  • ✓ Title, meta description, H1, and main copy are in the raw HTML
  • ✓ robots.txt does not disallow OAI-AdsBot or OAI-SearchBot
  • ✓ Page content genuinely matches your ad title, body, and context hints
  • ✓ No prohibited-category content (see our rejection reasons guide)
  • ✓ Redirect chain is short and the final URL is what you submitted

FAQ

Does OAI-AdsBot crawl my whole site?

No. It only visits URLs submitted as ad landing pages, on demand.

Is my content used to train OpenAI models?

No. OpenAI states OAI-AdsBot data is not used for model training.

How do I verify a request really is OAI-AdsBot?

Reverse DNS: the hostname should end in openai.com. There is no public IP list.
