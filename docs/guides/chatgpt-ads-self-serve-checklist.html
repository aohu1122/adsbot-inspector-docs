Self-Serve ChatGPT Ads: The 10-Point Launch Checklist

ChatGPT Ads opened self-serve buying with no minimum spend in May 2026, in 40+ countries. The barrier to entry is now one credit card — which is exactly why most wasted spend happens before the campaign even goes live. Run this checklist first.

A. Account & targeting (do once)

  1. 1. Business account verified. Set up billing before campaign day; rejected payment at launch loses your review slot.
  2. 2. Country availability confirmed. The platform rolled out through 2026 and coverage still varies — confirm your target market is live before writing copy.

B. Landing page (where ads die quietly)

  1. 3. Crawler can reach the page. Your WAF/CDN must not challenge OAI-AdsBot — see the Cloudflare guide if in doubt.
  2. 4. Content in raw HTML. Title, meta description, H1 and body copy must exist without JavaScript. Client-side rendered SPAs read as blank pages to the crawler.
  3. 5. robots.txt allows OAI-AdsBot and OAI-SearchBot. OpenAI's advertiser guidance requires both.
  4. 6. Policy scan clean. No prohibited categories, no OpenAI branding, no "guaranteed income / miracle cure / only 3 left" claim patterns. Details in 7 rejection reasons.
💡 Steps 3–6 are exactly what AdBot Inspector checks in one pass — a full report in ~10 seconds, free.

C. Creative & relevance

  1. 7. Ad mirrors the page. The core promise in your ad title/body must appear (in substance) in the page's H1/meta/copy. End-to-end consistency is a review criterion.
  2. 8. Context hints filled with real keywords. Don't leave the field empty or stuff it. Use the phrases your page genuinely targets — a good scanner can suggest them from your page content.

D. Money

  1. 9. Budget sized to the channel. Observed 2026 pricing runs roughly $25–60 CPM and $3–5 CPC depending on market. Size a test budget you can spend 3–5× before judging results.
  2. 10. Success metric written down. Before launch, write the number that decides scale-up vs. stop (signup, trial, purchase). Conversational ad slots don't behave like search — judge on your metric, not on CTR alone.

The 60-second version

Verified billing ✓ → page crawlable ✓ → policy clean ✓ → ad matches page ✓ → hints real ✓ → test budget + decision metric ✓. Anything uncertain in the middle four, run the free inspection and fix the report's action list first.
